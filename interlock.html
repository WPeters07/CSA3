🧱 Interlock

Cleaning and re-sanding interlock bricks to restore stability, appearance, and longevity.

Our interlock cleaning and re-sanding service is designed to fully restore both the appearance and structure of your pavers. We begin by removing built-up dirt, weeds, moss, and stains using a targeted treatment and controlled pressure washing process. Once the surface is properly cleaned, we re-fill all joints with high-quality polymeric sand, locking the pavers back into place and helping prevent future weed growth.

We service driveways, patios, walkways, and all interlocking stone surfaces, ensuring a consistent, even finish across the entire area.

This service is essential to perform regularly because over time, joint sand naturally erodes and organic growth takes over. Without proper maintenance, pavers can begin to shift, separate, and lose their clean, uniform look. Re-sanding restores stability, improves drainage, and keeps your interlock looking sharp and well-maintained.

We back every job with our 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you’re not completely happy with the results, we’ll come back and make it right at no additional cost.
