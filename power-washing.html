💧 Power Washing

Professional pressure washing for driveways, patios, decks, siding, and more.

At SPX Power Washing, our power washing service is designed to safely and effectively remove dirt, grime, algae, mold, and years of buildup from your property. We don’t just rely on pressure alone-our process starts with a plant-safe sodium hypochlorite solution that breaks down organic growth and stains at the source. We then follow up with carefully adjusted pressure levels to ensure a deep clean without damaging your surfaces.

We clean a wide range of exterior surfaces including driveways, sidewalks, patios, decks, fences, siding, brick, and more. Each material is treated with the appropriate method to ensure the best results while maintaining structural integrity.

Routine power washing is essential for maintaining both the appearance and longevity of your property. Organic buildup like mold and algae can cause staining, deterioration, and even safety hazards over time. Regular cleaning not only improves curb appeal but also helps prevent costly repairs down the line.

We stand behind every job with our 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you’re not happy with the results, we’ll return and make it right at no additional cost-no questions asked.
